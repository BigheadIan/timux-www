Step 3 · A 90-day path to adoption

See the capability and principles,
then see how 90 days take shape.

Choose one clearly scoped workflow. This reference cadence spans about 13 weeks and adapts to data, integration conditions, and an agreed scope—it is not a one-step replacement of every process.

01Weeks 1–2

Baseline & Workflow Confirmation

Find the right problem in real work.

Interview operators, managers, and system owners; review normal and exception cases; confirm data, handoffs, and current metrics.

You receive
Current-state workflow, prioritized needs, responsibilities, and acceptance method.
Stage gate
Shared agreement on the problem, scope, and success criteria.
02Weeks 3–6

Parallel Validation

Compare first, then decide whether to proceed.

Build a pilot using approved data in read-only analysis, compare it with existing human results, and identify errors, omissions, and exceptions.

You receive
Working pilot, result comparison, exception list, and adjustment rules.
Stage gate
Results for the selected use case meet an agreed quality threshold.
03Weeks 7–10

Controlled Execution

Enter real work within clear boundaries.

Limit users and permissions, connect a small-scale production flow, retain human approval for critical actions, and assign exception ownership.

You receive
Pilot workflow, permission and approval settings, and operation and exception records.
Stage gate
The team can use it, and exception handling and recovery have been validated.
04Weeks 11–13

Joint Acceptance

Let results determine the next step.

Review accuracy, wait time, realized value, and exceptions with business, finance, and technical owners.

You receive
Acceptance report, operating handoff, and recommendations to expand or adjust.
Stage gate
Expand only after meeting the criteria; otherwise revise validation or pause the operation.

Scale only after meeting the criteria; return to validation when data or rules are unstable.
