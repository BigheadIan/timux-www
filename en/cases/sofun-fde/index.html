THE DELIVERY LOOP

Start by completing one workflow—not by selling a large system.

Every stage has entry criteria and completion evidence. Permissions and scope do not expand automatically without joint confirmation.

01 / DISCOVER

Choose real work

Review normal and exception cases, roles, sources, waiting points, and the definition of done.

02 / VERIFY

Validate with data

Use approved information to test recommendations, sources, misses, and exceptions.

03 / EXECUTE

Execute with control

Limit users and actions; keep human approval for high-impact operations.

04 / ACCEPT

Accept jointly

Use accuracy, waiting time, exceptions, and actual value to decide what comes next.
